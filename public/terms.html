1. The service

We help passengers request seats and help station managers dispatch community vehicles. A booking is a request, not a guaranteed departure; times shown are estimates. Station managers can change assignments, delay or cancel trips. We are not liable for [LIABILITY TERMS: to be set by counsel], and nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

2. Your responsibilities

Give accurate details, arrive at the station by your stated time, cancel when plans change. Repeated no-shows (3 in 30 days by default) suspend booking for 14 days. Drivers must hold valid licence, insurance and roadworthiness documents; expired documents stop dispatch.

3. Personal data we process

Phone number, name, role, bookings and trip history, message delivery records, device/session information and security logs. Drivers also: licence details, vehicle details and uploaded documents. We collect only what is needed to run the service.

4. Why we use it (lawful basis)

To provide the service you asked for; to keep passengers and drivers safe; to meet legal duties; and, for WhatsApp updates and the terms themselves, with your consent. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM BASES.]

5. Who sees it

Your station manager and administrators; the driver assigned to your trip sees your booking reference, seats and station arrival time. Our messaging provider ([PROVIDER], [COUNTRY]) processes phone numbers and message text to deliver SMS/WhatsApp, which may involve transfer outside Ghana. We do not sell personal data.

6. WhatsApp

WhatsApp updates are optional and only sent if you switch them on in the app. You can switch them off at any time; SMS remains for important notices.

7. Retention

Messages are deleted after 90 days; audit and booking-history records after 2 years; sessions expire automatically; backups are kept for 28 days. Trip records needed for safety or disputes may be kept in anonymised form after account deletion.

8. Security

Encrypted connections, hashed one-time codes, staff PINs, access limited by role and station, activity logging, and regular backups.

9. Your rights

In the app you can download your data and delete your account. You may also ask us to correct your data, object to processing, or complain to the Data Protection Commission. Contact: [EMAIL].

10. Changes

If these terms change materially you will be asked to accept the new version before continuing.
